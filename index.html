About

I am a Mechatronics Engineering student and robotics researcher at Kennesaw State University. I am interested in collaborative, autonomous, and mobile robotics — particularly systems that have to sense, decide, and act in environments that do not cooperate.

Most of my work sits at the point where perception meets decision-making. I am currently researching uncertainty-aware trajectory prediction for robotic debris interception under Dr. Matt Marshall, building edge-deployed detection and forecasting systems on NVIDIA Jetson hardware so a robot can predict future movement while accounting for uncertainty rather than committing to a single prediction. In Dr. Muhammad Hassan Tanveer's AgriSys Lab I develop A2G, a collaborative aerial-ground search and rescue system, and SoilBus, an ad hoc soil monitoring network for precision agriculture.

Outside of research I design and build complete systems from the ground up. DARB, a drone and robotic arm docking system, is one I designed and built independently — mechanical design, embedded systems, and flight control in one project.

Before engineering, I served as a Ground Radio Repair Specialist in the Marine Corps Reserve. That work taught me how to read a complex system, troubleshoot under pressure, and keep equipment running. It carries over directly.
